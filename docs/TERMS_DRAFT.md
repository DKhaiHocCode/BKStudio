- In-stock figures: finished and ready to ship.

Painting a kit that you own is arranged with us directly, piece by piece. It is not ordered through this website and is not covered by these terms.

## 2. Prices and payment

- Prices are in USD. They do not include shipping, import taxes, GST or duties.
- We do not take card details on this website. We email you a payment link for Artistree or Shopee. The platform's own terms apply to that payment.
- In-stock figures: you pay 100% in one payment. We hold the figure for 48 hours from when we send the link.

## 3. Production queue and timing

We make a limited number of figures each month. Each figure takes one slot. Your place in the queue is set when we confirm your deposit, not when you place the order. The start month shown when you order is an estimate. We confirm the official month once your deposit is received. Dates are estimates, not guarantees. If we expect a delay, we will tell you.

## 4. Commissions

- We agree the details (character, pose, scale, colours) with you before you order.
- We email you when each stage is done. Work-in-progress photos are on your order page.
- Revisions are free until painting starts.
- If you do not reply while we are agreeing details or waiting for your feedback, we do not cancel your commission automatically. We follow up with you later. The payment deadlines in these terms still apply.

## 5. Balance payment

When your figure is finished, we email you a balance payment link. You have 30 days after we send the balance payment link to pay. We send reminders 7 days and 1 day before the deadline. If the balance is not paid in time, the order is forfeited, we keep the deposit, and we may sell the figure to someone else.

## 6. Cancellation and deposit

- Before you pay anything: if the deposit link or the in-stock payment link expires, the order is cancelled and nothing is charged.
- You cancel a made-to-order figure after your deposit is confirmed: the 50% deposit is not refunded, because we have reserved your slot and planned work for you.
- We cancel (for example we cannot make the figure, a print fails, or we stop taking orders): we refund your deposit in full.
- Commissions follow the same rules.
